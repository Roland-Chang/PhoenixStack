PhoenixStack Topology

L5 design draft, 2026-11-20. Every guest runs on pve01; each zone is a VLAN on its bridge vmbr0.

Decided in this picture

  • Three zones: DMZ (VLAN 200), Services (VLAN 100), Management (VLAN 10).
  • Two Traefik instances: a public one in the DMZ with only public routes, and an internal one in Services for LAN and VPN users.
  • Authentik lives in Services. The public Traefik asks it to log people in (forward-auth); its database never leaves Services.
  • One Docker host per zone, as VMs: docker01 (Services) and docker02 (DMZ), like docker-temp today.
  • Simple native services run as containers: Pi-hole, PostgreSQL, MariaDB.
  • Each GPU belongs to one VM and is shared by the containers inside it: P2000 for media, RTX 3070 for AI.
  • Addresses follow the original scheme: same last octet per tier in every zone, MAC 02:aa:bb:cc:XX:YY, VMID 1XXYY.

Still open

  • Cloudflare in front of the public web apps (proxy or tunnel), or DNS only?
  • Plex, Jellyfin, or both?
  • Which docker-temp apps are public (to docker02) and which internal (to docker01)?
  • AI tier code: proposed 12, address .131.
  • A second Pi-hole off pve01, so DNS survives a host reboot.
  • Monitoring (uptime and service alerts): which zone and when.
  • Install the 990 Pro drives (data pool) and the RTX 3070.

Still open

  1. Internet-facing hosts on the management network. The two forwards reach docker-temp and PLEX-TEMP on mgmt, with extra ports for docker-temp's current setup. Accepted until those apps move to the DMZ.
  2. Home and VPN reach the whole DMZ. Nothing is in it yet. When the first DMZ guest arrives, allow only the public ports plus SSH from admin sources.
  3. IOT DNS through Pi-hole (L5b): IOT's DHCP hands out Pi-hole's address, IOT → Services allows DNS to dns01 only, and outbound DNS (53, 853) to the internet is blocked.

Fixed on 2026-11-20: stale port forwards removed; Services DHCP limited to .150–.199; mgmt and IOT each moved to a zone of their own; every zone's access to the UDM limited to DNS, DHCP and ICMP; SSH is off on the UDM and the iDRAC; the Temp site-to-site VPN and unused port lists deleted; pve01's own admin allowlist narrowed to the same sources as the UDM (Young and Porter admin workstations, Teleport).
